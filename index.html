More about me ↗

I’m a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Computing at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. My research connects system dynamics, nonlinear optimal control, trajectory optimization, and machine learning.

I completed my PhD in Aeronautical and Aviation Engineering at PolyU in 2025, supervised by Professor Kam Hung Ng. I also hold an MSc in Mechanical Engineering from The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (2018) and a B.Eng. in Mechanical Engineering from The University of Hong Kong (2015).
