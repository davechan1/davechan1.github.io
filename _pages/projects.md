<article class="project" id="wind-estimation"><p class="eyebrow">01 / WIND ESTIMATION</p><h2>Using drones to read the wind</h2><p class="project-description">Drone-based wind-speed and direction estimation using onboard flight data, combining machine learning with system-dynamics approaches. This ongoing work brings estimation algorithms into real flight conditions.</p><ul><li>Validated third-party wind-estimation platforms, including AirData, with commercial-grade drones in wind-wall tests at the Guangdong Aerospace Research Academy (GARA).</li><li>Conducted validation flights at King’s Park Meteorological Station and Siu Ho Wan Wind automatic weather station, using DJI Mini 3 Pro and Mavic 4 Pro drones.</li><li>Developed an invariant extended Kalman filter (IEKF) using the symmetry-preserving properties of the dynamical system to estimate wind speed and direction.</li><li>Collaborated closely with the Hong Kong Observatory (HKO) and GARA.</li></ul><div class="project-hero">

<article class="project" id="motion-planning"><p class="eyebrow">03 / TRAJECTORY OPTIMIZATION</p><h2>Time-optimal paths through the city</h2><p class="project-description">A UAV motion-planning framework based on a novel curve-framing method and spatial reformulation, applied to last-mile delivery and cable-suspended payload quadcopters.</p><ul><li>Model the urban environment from building and structure data, including Hong Kong Lands Department and OpenStreetMap data.</li><li>Convert the environment to a grid map and compute a predefined or real-time global path. Reformulate vehicle position and velocity with a Gravity Normal frame.</li><li>Generate a flight corridor and refine it to eliminate vertical and lateral obstacle intrusions.</li><li>Use direct multiple shooting to optimize flight time with initial and terminal constraints, dynamic constraints, corridor-based state bounds, and control bounds.</li><li>The offline trajectory module assumes instantaneous controller response; the portfolio notes the resulting discrepancy between planned and actual performance.</li></ul><div class="project-hero">

<article class="project" id="physics-informed"><p class="eyebrow">04 / LEARNING SYSTEM DYNAMICS</p><h2>Physics-informed learning for suspended payloads</h2><p class="project-description">A Physics-Informed Attention Temporal Convolutional Network (PIATCN) for modelling cable-suspended quadcopters. The model combines temporal learning, attention, and physical constraints.</p><ul><li>Capture complex nonlinear behaviour while encouraging physically consistent predictions.</li><li>Use a temporal convolutional network with a Bahdanau attention layer and physics-informed constraints to reduce required training data.</li><li>Generate simulated flights in PX4 software-in-the-loop (SITL).</li><li>The portfolio reports improved performance over LSTM, attention LSTM, and baseline TCN models.</li></ul><div class="project-hero">
